Privacy policy for TellWell for Android

Effective 18 November 2026.

TellWell is a voice typing keyboard. It turns what you say into text using speech recognition and cleanup models that run on your phone. TellWell does not collect, store or share any of your data.

Your voice and text

  • The microphone is used only while TellWell's keyboard is on screen and listening: from when you switch to it until you tap to finish.
  • The recording is processed on your phone, in memory. It is never saved to a file and never sent anywhere.
  • The resulting text is typed into the field you are using. TellWell does not keep a copy.
  • To decide whether to add a space, TellWell reads the one character before the cursor. It reads nothing else from your apps.
  • TellWell does not work in password fields.

What TellWell does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising ID.
  • No crash reporting by the app.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos or files.

The models

TellWell needs its speech and cleanup models on your phone. The version from Google Play has no internet permission: Google Play delivers the models, under Google's own privacy policy. The version from GitHub uses the internet once, to download the models from GitHub (or from Hugging Face if GitHub is unavailable); like any download, those sites see your IP address. Nothing about you or your use of TellWell is sent in either case.

Data stored on your phone

TellWell stores the models and a few settings (such as whether this phone's NPU works) in its private app storage. Uninstalling TellWell removes them.

Google Play

If you install TellWell from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own policies, for example crash reports you choose to share with developers. TellWell itself adds nothing to this.

Children

TellWell is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Open source

TellWell is open source, so you can check all of the above in its source code.
