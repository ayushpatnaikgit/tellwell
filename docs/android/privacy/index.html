Privacy policy for TellWell for Android

Effective 27 November 2026.

TellWell is a voice typing keyboard. It turns what you say into text using speech recognition and cleanup models that run on your phone. TellWell does not collect, store or share any of your data. This policy covers both TellWell (English) and TellWell Multilingual (English and Hindi); "TellWell" means either.

Your voice and text

  • The microphone is used only while TellWell's keyboard is on screen and listening: from when you switch to it until you tap to finish.
  • The recording is processed on your phone, in memory. It is never saved to a file and never sent anywhere.
  • The resulting text is typed into the field you are using. TellWell does not keep a copy.
  • To spell names correctly and to decide whether to add a space, TellWell reads the text around the cursor in the field you are typing into (up to about 2,000 characters before it and 500 after). It is used in memory for that one dictation, never saved and never sent anywhere. TellWell skips this in fields that ask keyboards not to learn from them, such as incognito tabs. It reads nothing else from your apps.
  • TellWell does not work in password fields.

Contact names (optional)

This is off unless you turn it on in TellWell. If you do, TellWell reads your contacts' names (names only: no phone numbers, email addresses or other details) to help spell the names you say. They are kept in memory on your phone, never saved by TellWell and never sent anywhere. You can turn it off in TellWell, or remove the permission in Android's settings, at any time.

What TellWell does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or advertising ID.
  • No crash reporting by the app.
  • No access to your location, photos or files, and none to your contacts unless you turn on contact names.

The models

TellWell needs its speech and cleanup models on your phone. The version from Google Play has no internet permission: Google Play delivers the models, under Google's own privacy policy. The version from GitHub uses the internet once, to download the models from GitHub (or from Hugging Face if GitHub is unavailable); like any download, those sites see your IP address. Nothing about you or your use of TellWell is sent in either case.

TellWell Multilingual, from Google Play or GitHub, also uses the internet to download the models for a language you add (such as Hindi), from TellWell's model releases on GitHub. It downloads them only when you add a language or change its model size. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any download. TellWell Multilingual sends nothing else over the internet: your voice and text never leave your phone.

Data stored on your phone

TellWell stores the models and a few settings (such as whether this phone's NPU works, and in TellWell Multilingual the languages you added and which one you last used) in its private app storage. Removing a language deletes its models; uninstalling TellWell removes everything.

Google Play

If you install TellWell from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own policies, for example crash reports you choose to share with developers. TellWell itself adds nothing to this.

Children

TellWell is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Open source

TellWell is open source, so you can check all of the above in its source code.
