TellWell / Free Wispr Flow alternative

Free · open source · offline

A free Wispr Flow alternative for Mac.

TellWell is a free, open-source Wispr Flow alternative for Mac. Like Wispr Flow, you hold a key, speak, and clean text appears in any app. Unlike Wispr Flow, TellWell has no paid plan and no weekly word limit, and speech recognition and cleanup run entirely on your Mac, so it works offline and nothing you say is uploaded. Wispr Flow is the better fit if you need Windows, iPhone or Android, or languages other than English.

Which one to choose

Choose TellWell if

  • you dictate on a Mac and don't want a subscription or a word limit
  • what you say must stay on your Mac: client work, health, legal, code
  • you work offline, on planes or on unreliable connections
  • your writing is full of names and terms that speech models mishear
  • you want software you can read and change

Choose Wispr Flow if

  • you need dictation on Windows, iPhone or Android
  • you dictate in languages other than English
  • your team needs admin controls

Context-aware, without sending your screen anywhere

TellWell reads the unusual words on your screen, such as names, product terms and code identifiers, with macOS's built-in text recognition on your Mac, and uses your vocabulary list. When a word you said sounds like one of them, it is spelled the way it appears on screen. It never inserts a word you didn't say. Nothing it reads is stored or uploaded. Install TellWell

One command in Terminal installs TellWell and its on-device engine (models download once, about 2 GB), starts it at login and connects the two. Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later.

curl -fsSL https://ayushpatnaikgit.github.io/tellwell/install.sh | sh

Then open TellWell, allow Microphone and Accessibility, and press the key you want to talk with.

Questions about switching from Wispr Flow

Is Wispr Flow free?

Wispr Flow has a free plan with "limited words per week". Its Pro plan costs $15 per user per month, or $12 per user per month billed annually, according to wisprflow.ai/pricing on 14 November 2026.

Is TellWell really free?

Yes. TellWell is open source under the MIT licence. There is no paid plan, no word limit, no account and no API key.

Does TellWell work offline?

Yes. Speech recognition runs on the Apple Neural Engine and cleanup runs on a small model on your Mac, so dictation works without an internet connection once TellWell is installed.

Does TellWell work on Windows, iPhone or Android?

No. TellWell runs on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) with macOS 14 or later. If you need dictation on other devices, Wispr Flow supports Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.

Which languages does TellWell support?

TellWell is tuned for English. Wispr Flow lists support for more than 100 languages.

How do I switch from Wispr Flow to TellWell?

Run the one-line installer in Terminal, open TellWell, allow Microphone and Accessibility, and press the key you want to talk with. You can keep Wispr Flow installed while you try it; give each a different shortcut.
